# Products

## Fixed-Rate Loan

Bank asset. Future cash flows are positive from the bank liquidity perspective.

Initial implementation:

- bullet principal repayment at maturity;
- fixed annual nominal rate;
- ACT/365 interest accrual;
- interest payment frequencies: at maturity, monthly, quarterly;
- no amortization;
- no prepayment;
- no delinquency/default behavior;
- no fee income.

## Fixed-Rate Deposit

Bank liability. Future cash flows are negative from the bank liquidity perspective.

Initial implementation:

- bullet principal repayment at maturity;
- fixed annual nominal rate;
- ACT/365 interest accrual;
- interest payment frequencies: at maturity, monthly, quarterly;
- no early withdrawal;
- no rollover;
- no behavioral maturity.
